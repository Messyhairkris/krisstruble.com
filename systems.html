Systems & Documentation

I am constantly learning new processes, tools, and information. Building systems and documenting what I learn is how I keep that knowledge from disappearing.

I write SOPs, how-to guides, templates, and small automation tools so knowledge can be shared instead of staying in one person’s head. They make unfamiliar work easier to approach, easier to teach, and easier to repeat.

Systems for repeatable work

From concept to shelf: a kit development checklist

A repeatable process for taking a product from the initial idea through sourcing, production, launch, and review. It usually takes 6–12 weeks.

  1. ConceptNeed, competitors, price point
  2. SourcingQuotes, samples, minimum orders
  3. FinancialsCost, $1/min labor, margin, owner approval
  4. PackagingLabels, instructions, design review
  5. OrderingTracking, inspection, receiving
  6. Assembly prepWorkspace, staff instructions
  7. Build & testTime the first 5–10 kits for real labor cost
  8. InventorySKU, POS, barcodes, tags
  9. LaunchDisplay, staff briefing, web listing
  10. ReviewSales, feedback, reorder decisions
