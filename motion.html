Motion, Prototypes & Sets

I like learning tools by pushing them past the obvious use case. Animation and prototyping give me a way to explore not just what something looks like, but how it behaves.

  • Prototyping
  • Animation
  • 3D

Go Take a Hike

A Figma prototype about transitions and motion.

This was only my second Figma project. I wanted to see how far I could push the prototype, so I built a small hiking experience around transitions, movement, and timing.

The project eventually led to a conversation with Figma’s product team about how people use Figma for vector editing and illustration. They asked me to share recent work, demonstrate my workflow, and create something in Figma alongside my preferred illustration tool.

The conversation was a reminder of something I enjoy about learning software: using a tool seriously enough to discover where it works well, where it gets in the way, and what I wish it could do.

Spooky Reading

A short character animation built as another experiment in motion.

I created the character, character sheet, and animation, using the project to explore how a simple illustrated character could communicate personality through movement.

CHARGED

A 3D stage concept for a live game show pitch.

This was a freelance commission from earlier in my career. I designed and modeled a proposed stage for a live game show, turning a written concept into a visual environment that could be presented and discussed.

It is a different kind of work from the other projects on this page, but it shows an earlier part of my practice: using 3D tools to make an idea concrete enough for other people to react to.
